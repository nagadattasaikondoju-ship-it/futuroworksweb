# Futuro Works — Website

Website for **Futuro Works**, a Hyderabad-based AVCGI (Animation, Visual Graphics, Comics, Interactive Media) education and creative studio founded by **M. Sri Vatsha**.

Tagline: **"Future Together"**
Positioning line: *"We don't just tell stories. We build worlds."*

---

## Brand

- **Categories:** Animation · VFX · Graphics · Gaming · Comics · Photography · Videography · Cinematography
- **Palette:** Black background, bright yellow accent, white text
- **Logo:** Black circular badge, "FUTURO WORKS" wordmark in yellow
- **Self-description:** "A creative engine that powers brands, filmmakers, gamers, and dreamers"

---

## Site Sections

1. **Hero** — logo, tagline, category strip, CTA to connect (WhatsApp community / Linktree)
2. **We Are Futuro Works** — intro to the studio as an "AVCGI powerhouse," M. Sri Vatsha's founder story
3. **Programs** — four pillars, each with student count + tools taught (see Content Inventory below)
4. **Why AVCGI? Why Now?** — market rationale / industry pitch
5. **Market** — industry size stats
6. **What We Do** — core service list
7. **Resources** — Education & Training, Mentorship, Industry Collaborations, Global Exposure
8. **Meet the Team** — founder + manager profiles
9. **Our Partners** — partner logos
10. **Testimonials** — certificate/proof images
11. **Connect** — WhatsApp community QR, Linktree QR
